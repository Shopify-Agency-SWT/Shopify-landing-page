# Agency build plan: Linear issues (team SHO)

Grouped by project. Each line: **title**: what "done" means.

## 1. Foundation
1. **Choose agency name and logo**: final name (no "Shopify" in the brand, per Shopify's trademark rules), logo, colours.
2. **Buy domain and set up business email**: domain bought, hello@ / sales@ mailboxes working.
3. **Register the business and tax**: company or proprietorship registered, GST (India) set up, business bank account open.
4. **Create Shopify Partner account**: Partner account live, BlinkDrop and Swayogi linked as managed stores, development stores available.
5. **Set up internal tools**: Linear, Google Drive folders, shared password manager, invoicing tool, WhatsApp Business.

## 2. Services, packages & pricing
6. **Write the final list of services**: 10 services with a one-paragraph scope each (what's included and what isn't).
7. **Create packages**: e.g. Starter store, Growth store, Custom/Plus; Care plan; Growth retainer.
8. **Set prices**: price for each package in INR, GBP and USD; hourly rate for extra work.
9. **Proposal template**: reusable proposal (Google Doc) with scope, timeline, price and terms.
10. **Contract and statement-of-work template**: payment terms (e.g. 50% upfront), revisions, ownership, support period.
11. **Client onboarding checklist**: access needed (collaborator request, domain, Meta/Google), kickoff call agenda, content checklist.

## 3. Agency website
12. **Finish home page content**: real name, email, prices decision, final text approved.
13. **Service pages (one per service)**: 10 pages with what we do, process, FAQs and a call-to-action.
14. **Case study pages: BlinkDrop and Swayogi**: problem, what we built, screenshots, results.
15. **About page**: who we are, team, why us.
16. **Working contact form**: form sends to email/CRM, plus WhatsApp button and Calendly booking link.
17. **SEO setup for the agency site**: titles, meta descriptions, sitemap, robots, Organization and Service structured data.
18. **Analytics and pixels**: GA4, Meta pixel, Google Search Console; track form submits and call bookings.
19. **Deploy to Vercel with the domain**: live on the real domain with HTTPS.
20. **Blog section**: blog set up for SEO articles.

## 4. Apps & reusable products
21. **Reusable theme starter**: our own starter theme (based on Dawn) with ready sections we use on every build.
22. **Publish the Special Offers app**: turn the BlinkDrop offers app into a public Shopify App Store app.
23. **Publish the Live Carts app**: same for the live carts app.
24. **Shopify app template**: our own starter for new custom apps (auth, billing, admin UI).
25. **Headless storefront starter**: Next.js + Storefront API starter from the BlinkDrop custom site.
26. **Migration toolkit**: scripts to move products, customers, orders and redirects from WooCommerce/Wix/Magento.

## 5. Marketing & outreach
27. **Shopify Partner Directory listing**: profile with services, case studies and reviews.
28. **Google Business Profile**: listing verified, photos, services, first reviews from BlinkDrop and Swayogi.
29. **LinkedIn company page and founder posts**: page live, 3 posts a week plan.
30. **Instagram page and content calendar**: page live, 1-month calendar (before/after, tips, case studies).
31. **Agency listings**: Clutch, GoodFirms, DesignRush, Upwork and Fiverr profiles.
32. **Free store audit offer**: an audit checklist and landing page to get leads ("Free Shopify store audit").
33. **Outreach list of target brands**: 200 Shopify stores (D2C India, UK small brands) with weak sites or no ads.
34. **Cold email and LinkedIn sequence**: 3-step message sequence plus tracking sheet.
35. **Ads for the agency**: small Meta and Google ads test for "Shopify developer" / "Shopify agency" searches.
36. **Referral programme**: reward for clients and freelancers who refer new clients.
37. **SEO articles**: first 10 blog posts (e.g. "Shopify store cost in India", "WooCommerce to Shopify migration").

## 6. Delivery & operations
38. **Project workflow in Linear**: template project for each client build (stages, checklists).
39. **Monthly client report template**: sales, traffic, ads and SEO in plain words.
40. **Support and care-plan process**: how requests come in, response times, hour tracking.
41. **Freelancer and hiring bench**: list of designers, developers and ad specialists we can call on.
